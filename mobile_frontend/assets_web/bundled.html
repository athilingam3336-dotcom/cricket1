GOVERNING CRICKET BODY

The Heritage of Cricket in Virudhunagar

The Cricket Federation of Virudhunagar District (CFVD) is the sole governing body for the sport of cricket within Virudhunagar revenue district, proudly affiliated with the Tamil Nadu Cricket Association (TNCA).

With roots deeply anchored in sportsmanship and youth development, our emblem features the world-renowned temple gopuram of Srivilliputhur (the state emblem of Tamil Nadu), reflecting the pride and spiritual resilience of our athletes.

Fair Play & Ethics

Strict implementation of BCCI Anti-Corruption & Code of Conduct rules.

Grassroots Talent Scouting

Annual camps identifying rural talent across 8 taluks for TNCA representation.

First-Class Standard Pitches

BCCI-curated turf wickets at District Sports Complex and Sivakasi.
