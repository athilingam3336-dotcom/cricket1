GOVERNING CRICKET BODY

The Heritage of Cricket in Virudhunagar

The Cricket Federation of Virudhunagar District (CFVD) is the official recognized governing body for the sport of cricket within Virudhunagar revenue district.

With roots deeply anchored in sportsmanship, collegiate competition, and youth development, our emblem features the world-renowned temple gopuram of Srivilliputhur (the state emblem of Tamil Nadu), reflecting the pride and resilience of our cricketers.

Fair Play & Ethics

Strict implementation of Federation Code of Conduct and Anti-Corruption rules.

Grassroots & Collegiate Scouting

Annual leagues identifying talent across 8 taluks, schools, and affiliated colleges.

First-Class Standard Pitches

Professional red-soil turf wickets at District Sports Complex, Sivakasi, and Rajapalayam.
